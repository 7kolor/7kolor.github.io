## What we do

We monitor public developer communities and publish a weekly digest of
noteworthy signals: emerging pain points, trending tools, and shifts in
developer attention. Every item is linked to its public source; our value
add is selection, context, and judgment.

我们在公开开发者社区中持续观察，每周发布值得注意的信号：
新出现的痛点、升温的工具、注意力的迁移。每条信号均可回溯到公开来源，
我们的价值在于筛选、 context 与判断。

## Methodology

- Sources: public posts and discussions on Hacker News, Reddit, Product Hunt, GitHub, DEV.
- Signals are selected by a combination of quantitative attention metrics and editorial review.
- We only use publicly available content. No private data, no paywalled sources.

- 数据来源：上述平台的公开帖子与讨论。
- 筛选方式：量化热度指标 + 人工研判。
- 只使用公开内容，不涉及任何私有数据。
